# Week 3 — Full Hand Mechanical Assembly

## Goals

- Complete the mechanical assembly of the entire hand.
- Mount all servos in their locations.
- Verify basic mechanical motion before electronics.

## Tasks

1. **Print or unpack remaining parts**
   - Print or organize all remaining fingers, palm, and mounts.
   - Inspect parts for defects.

2. **Assemble the hand**
   - Follow the Amazing Hand assembly guide.
   - Install all fingers onto the palm.
   - Mount all servos in their designated positions.

3. **Route tendons or linkages**
   - Attach tendons or mechanical linkages as specified.
   - Ensure each finger can move through its full range.
   - Adjust tension as needed.

4. **Mechanical testing**
   - Manually move each finger to confirm smooth motion.
   - Note any binding or stiff joints and fix them.

5. **Update your repo**
   - Add photos of the assembled hand to `docs/` or a `media/` folder.
   - Update `docs/notes.md` with any mechanical issues and how you solved them.

## Deliverables

- Fully assembled mechanical hand with all servos mounted.
- Photos and notes in the repo.

## Time Estimate

- 8–10 hours total.

---

## Self-check

- Can all fingers move freely by hand?
- Are all servos securely mounted?
- Do you feel confident that the mechanical side is ready for wiring?
