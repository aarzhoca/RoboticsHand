# Week 12 — Final Demo and Next Steps

## Goals

- Prepare a final demonstration of the project.
- Review what you learned.
- Plan possible improvements or a second version.

## Tasks

1. **Prepare a demo**
   - Choose 2–3 impressive behaviors:
     - Teleoperated grasping of different objects.
     - Predefined grasp patterns (open, close, custom poses).
     - Any special gestures or motions you designed.
   - Practice the demo sequence to ensure it runs smoothly.

2. **Record or present**
   - Record a short video (2–5 minutes) showing:
     - The hand in action.
     - Teleoperation with the glove.
     - A brief explanation of the system.
   - Alternatively, prepare a live demo if presenting in person.

3. **Review and reflect**
   - Write a short reflection in `docs/notes.md` or a new `docs/retrospective.md`:
     - What worked well.
     - What was difficult.
     - What you would change in a second version.
   - List concrete next steps, such as:
     - Wireless control.
     - Stronger servos or different hand design.
     - More advanced control algorithms.

4. **Finalize the repo**
   - Ensure:
     - `README.md` is complete and clear.
     - All key docs are linked.
     - Code is clean and builds without errors.
   - Add a short “Getting Started” section if needed.

5. **Share your work**
   - Share the repo link with your mentor, classmates, or online communities.
   - Optionally write a short blog post or social media post about your project.

## Deliverables

- A final demo (video or live).
- A short retrospective and next-steps plan.
- A polished, public-ready GitHub repository.

## Time Estimate

- 6–8 hours total.

---

## Self-check

- Are you proud of the final system and demo?
- Can you clearly explain what you built and how it works?
- Do you have a clear idea of what you want to do next in robotics?
